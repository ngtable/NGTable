# Licensing

NGTable's source is public, and it is free for noncommercial use. Using it in
a business needs a commercial license. This page explains which one you need;
the licenses themselves are [LICENSE](LICENSE),
[LICENSE-NONCOMMERCIAL.md](LICENSE-NONCOMMERCIAL.md) and the commercial
license agreement you get when you buy one.

## Free: the PolyForm Noncommercial License

[PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0)
lets you use, change and share NGTable for any noncommercial purpose:

- personal projects, hobbies and study, without any anticipated commercial
  application;
- research, experiments and testing for the benefit of public knowledge;
- use by charitable organizations, educational institutions (schools,
  universities), public research organizations, public safety or health
  organizations, environmental protection organizations and government
  institutions, however they are funded.

If you share NGTable or something built on it, pass on the license and the
`Required Notice:` line of [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Paid: a commercial license

Any other use needs a commercial license, for example:

- an app you sell, or a free app of a business (in the App Store or not);
- an internal tool of a company, even one nobody outside it sees;
- work for a client, as a freelancer, a contractor or an agency;
- a product or SDK of yours that includes NGTable.

A commercial license is per developer: everyone who writes or debugs code
that uses NGTable needs one. Build machines and testers don't.

### Versions

A commercial license covers a major version of NGTable: a license for 1.x
covers 1.0, 1.1, 1.2 and every other 1.x release, for as long as you use them.
Apps you ship with a licensed version stay licensed: you can keep selling
and updating them with that version without paying again. A later major
version (2.x) needs an upgrade.

`NGTableView.version` tells which version your app has.

### Trying it out

A business may try NGTable for up to 30 days before buying, as long as it
doesn't ship it or use it in production meanwhile.

### Open-source projects

If your app is open source as a whole (under a license the Open Source
Initiative approves) and published, write to us: we give open-source projects
a free commercial license.

## NGTableICU

The optional NGTableICU library is licensed the same way. The ICU it is built
with, `Vendor/CollationICU`, isn't: it is ICU (the Unicode license) and code of
swift-foundation-icu (the Apache License 2.0), under their licenses
(`Vendor/CollationICU/LICENSE.md`).

## Contributions

NGTable doesn't take pull requests for now: all its code has one author, so it
can be licensed both ways. Bug reports, questions and ideas are welcome as
issues (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
